Rapla plans whatever has to be in one place at one time. The screenshots show the Rapla 3 web app with sample data, in its German interface.

## University and school

A small faculty with two courses of study: timetables per course and per room, lectures repeating over the semester, conflicts when a room or lecturer is booked twice — plus loanable equipment such as beamers, cameras and bicycles, and room requests that the timetable office approves in the desktop client.

![Week view of one course with its lectures and an exam Q&A](/images/use-cases/university-week.png)

![Equipment loans in the week view: beamers, laptops, cameras and bicycles](/images/use-cases/university-loans.png)

## Equipment loans

A media centre lends cameras, projectors and a van. Desk staff see availability over several weeks, and a loan that collides with another one is flagged before it is saved.

![Month view of camera and van loans](/images/use-cases/loans-availability.png)

![Check before saving: the camera is already booked at that time](/images/use-cases/loans-conflict.png)

## Seminar house

Multi-day seminars in seminar rooms and guest rooms, coloured by kind — yoga, cooking, leadership. The house manager sees everything, trainers see their own seminars, and the programme for the next months is one grouped table.

![Month grid with multi-day seminars coloured by kind](/images/use-cases/seminar-month.png)

![Programme grouped by month with kind, participants and trainer](/images/use-cases/seminar-programme.png)

## Duty roster

A hospital with two wards, teams per ward and four shifts a day. Planners see who is on when and handle swaps by changing a single shift, team members see their own shifts, and a read-only station screen shows today's shifts on the ward.

![Week view by person with night, morning and afternoon shifts](/images/use-cases/roster-week.png)

![Read-only station screen with today's shifts on one ward](/images/use-cases/roster-station.png)
