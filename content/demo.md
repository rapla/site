## Rapla 2 demos

The Rapla 2 demos are the campus installations. They are reset every night at 11 pm CET — feel free to play around. User names for each demo are listed on the {{< wiki "Home" "wiki start page" >}}.

{{< cards >}}
{{< card "Campus A — empty" "https://rapla-campus.dhbw-karlsruhe.de/rapla_a" >}}An empty installation with two resource types and one resource.{{< /card >}}
{{< card "Campus B — first steps" "https://rapla-campus.dhbw-karlsruhe.de/rapla_b" >}}A few users, resources and two event types: lecture and examination.{{< /card >}}
{{< card "Campus C — two institutes" "https://rapla-campus.dhbw-karlsruhe.de/rapla_c" >}}Planners for a maths and a physics institute, with permissions per event type.{{< /card >}}
